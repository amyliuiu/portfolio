About

Since picking up a crayon at age four, I've been hooked on the creative process. My soul flourished through fine art from elementary to high school. I experimented with every medium I could obtain: watercolor (my favorite), pastels, polymer clay, ink, and more.

I wanted to keep in touch with my creative side during college, so I joined Design for America. I spent my first year at USC learning how to use Figma and completing my first case study. It was love at first prototype. Since then, I've designed pro-bono for the Los Angeles community at Code the Change and Innovative Design.

I had two stints at Apple while I was a student. After graduating I moved to NYC to work at Shopify, where I get to design for one of my favorite pastimes—shopping.
